DAILY SELF-CARE

WHAT IS NORMAL
Some pain around your incision, low appetite, trouble sleeping, tiredness, mood swings, and swelling in the leg where the vein was taken can be normal for several weeks.

CALL 911 RIGHT AWAY IF YOU HAVE
- Chest pain or angina symptoms
- Shortness of breath that does not get better with rest
- Dizziness or fainting
- Sudden weakness or numbness in your face, arm or leg, trouble speaking, or a sudden severe headache
- A very fast, very slow or irregular heartbeat
- Heavy sweating for no reason

- Redness, swelling, bleeding, oozing, or opening at any incision
- Weight gain of 2 pounds or more in one day, or 5 pounds or more in one week
- More swelling in your legs, especially your right leg (where no vein was taken)
- A cough that will not go away, or coughing up blood or yellow/green mucus
- Sharp chest pain when you breathe in deeply, or a clicking feeling in your breastbone
- Trouble taking any of your heart medicines
